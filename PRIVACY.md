# Biggofiles privacy

Biggofiles is made by OG STUDIO. The public privacy policy is available at
[ogstudioapps.com/bf/pp.html](https://ogstudioapps.com/bf/pp.html).

This document describes the behavior of this source release:

- Scans run locally and read directory metadata such as names, paths, sizes, attributes, and modification times.
- Scan results are held in memory. They are not uploaded or automatically saved.
- CSV results and scan notes are written only when you choose an export destination.
- The application has no analytics, advertising, account system, update polling, or network requests.
- Website and privacy buttons open the configured URL in your browser only when clicked. The destination website has its own privacy behavior.
- File actions use Windows Explorer, the clipboard, and the Recycle Bin as explicitly requested. These Windows components may keep their own history or metadata.

Closing the app discards its in-memory scan. Exports remain at the location you selected until you remove them.
